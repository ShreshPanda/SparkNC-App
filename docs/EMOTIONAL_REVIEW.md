## Microcopy review

- All primary CTAs now use action verbs (`Send`, `Submit Feedback`, `Submit Idea`, `Generate Insights`).
- Empty states use encouraging language instead of dead-ends.
- Hero cards give an immediate sense of context and progress.

## Animation and delight

- `FadeIn` is used on leadership, community, and portfolio screens for a calm, premium feel.
- `SparkButton` press-scale animation gives tactile feedback.
- `TodaysSpark` insight rotation fades in smoothly.

## Accessibility note

- Screen-reader labels on auth, ambassador, and message actions ensure users of all abilities feel included.
