# Pilot Ambassador Guide

## Your role

As a SparkNC ambassador, you support assigned students, answer questions, and help them stay engaged.

## Getting started

1. Log in with your assigned `ambassador` role.
2. Open the **Ambassador** tab to see your students.
3. Review support status, reasons, and suggested actions.

## Daily workflow

1. Check the **Ambassador Dashboard** for students who need help.
2. Send messages or schedule check-ins.
3. Log feedback after interactions.
4. Submit **Ambassador Feedback** for program-level issues or wins.

## Student support

- Use `/ambassador/student-support` to view at-risk and inactive students.
- Reach out proactively.
- Celebrate progress with Spark Moments.

## Recognition

Ambassadors earn recognition through leadership metrics and community impact. Your contributions are tracked in the leadership dashboard.

## Escalation

If a student needs admin intervention, flag the case through the Ambassador Feedback screen.
