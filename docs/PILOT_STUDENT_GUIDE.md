# Pilot Student Guide

## Welcome to SparkNC

SparkNC helps you set goals, complete tasks, track growth, connect with peers, and get AI support.

## First five minutes

1. Open the app and sign up.
2. Tell SparkNC your goals, interests, growth areas, and preferred support style.
3. View your personalized dashboard.
4. Add or pick your first task.
5. Set your first goal.

## Core screens

- **Dashboard** — XP, streak, active tasks, goals, and upcoming events.
- **Tasks** — daily actions and to-dos.
- **Goals** — bigger milestones with progress.
- **Journey** — your story over time.
- **Portfolio** — showcase projects, achievements, and skills.
- **AI** — ask SparkNC for help, feedback, or motivation.
- **Community** — join groups, post updates, and help classmates.

## Earning XP and achievements

- Complete tasks and goals.
- Attend events.
- Engage in the community.
- Maintain streaks.

Your Spark Moment celebrations will appear when you hit milestones.

## Getting help

- Ask the AI companion.
- Message your ambassador if one is assigned.
- Submit feedback from the Feedback screen.
